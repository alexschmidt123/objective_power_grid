F. Research conclusions and limits

  1. The strongest completed MoE variant is ResMoE-sBOED at 10.84765 nats. This is a one-training-seed development result.
  2. Compared with the original DAD reference, the observed difference is +1.08099 nats; residual methods include an extra pretrained training budget.
  3. Historical SwitchMoE improves numerically on ordinary MoE, but also used a different sharpening schedule. Its hard learned gate always requests rerouting; always-reroute EIG is identical. The historical improvement does not demonstrate useful temporal persistence.
  4. Compared with the original DAD-MLP checkpoint, the observed difference is +0.18505 nats.
  5. Equal-additional-budget continued DAD-MLP is pending, so extra training remains an unresolved explanation.
  6. ResMoE learned routing and forced dominant-expert routing currently match within paired uncertainty. Proposal disagreement or higher overall EIG alone does not establish beneficial expert specialization. Forced routing may induce unfamiliar histories.
  • One independent training seed; MC intervals are not training-seed variation
  • Historical SwitchMoE uses legacy gamma schedule; original slower MoE and residual variants use30/95 cosine
  • Residual models include pretrained base effort; continued DAD-MLP is required for attribution
  • DAD-MLP input772 versus full-MoE input193; matched193 control reported separately
  • External RL-sBOED is an existing documented two-Q adaptation; exact published reproduction is not established
  • Forced-routing failures may reflect out-of-distribution histories

Incomplete experiments and the one controlled intervention remain pending where indicated. Expert proposal differences alone do not establish useful specialization.
