# Baseline fidelity: evidence and limits

The local ScoreBED vendor tree matches every SHA256 in its official pinned-source provenance (commit cc8e996e3ee3237ab4c9bd3ef80e2e4b4aa9cee4). Native conservative-transformer score network, DAD-style policy, conditional-score matching target and native score policy-gradient code remain unchanged. The adapter maps both score-training designs and policy actions to [-4,4]². The bounded validation export correction remains present. Budget overrides, minibatch64 and one restart are explicit resource-saving departures from upstream defaults; this is a bounded, fixed-budget adaptation, not an exact paper reproduction. Audit did not establish a new training bug or justify a five-seed ScoreBED rerun. Existing poor outcomes remain included. The completed crossing establishes bundle dependence, not globally inferior score accuracy or a specific sampler defect.

Simplified RL-sBOED remains explicitly simplified: gamma1, two Q networks and two replay-gradient cycles are historical documented settings, not a faithful replacement of every published algorithmic detail. No replacement learner is introduced here. DAD retains its native sum256/16 encoder and linear emitter; MoE retains64/64tanh experts. vsOED uses its native variational actor-critic framework, bounded benchmark adaptation and published incremental discount0.9. Training durations and likelihood work differ and must be reported.

All five residual seed pairs share byte-identical seed-specific base checkpoints and satisfy500k+500k accounting. Original MoE and the journal campaign's SwitchMoE both use30%/95% routing schedules for all five seeds; the older SwitchMoE10.23554 checkpoint is a distinct, confounded historical result. No new routing-adoption decision has been made.

Remaining limits: baseline numerical tests are recorded separately; this source audit is not a fresh independent reproduction of each source paper. Complete historical hardware/runtime metadata may be incomplete. Do not fill missing costs with estimates or certify journal readiness solely from this audit.
