- **Random:** no policy training; one paired development/final reference. The design sampler is the existing standard normal conditioned on[-4,4]², not a silently substituted uniform baseline.
- **Optimized Fixed:** native joint fixed-design sPCE optimization; final1M budget, recorded fixed learning rate. A nonadaptive learned design, not a random sequence.
- **DAD:** pinned ae-foster/dad architecture reference4b1008174e1531d1f14601d83cef481c0f586f36; sum256/16 history encoder and original linear emitter. PureTorch adapter, bounded feasible map, common sPCE and fixed1M budget are explicit adaptations. It was not replaced with MoE's64/64tanh head. Architecture source provenance does not imply byte-identical training-framework reproduction.

Shared history normalization and independent stochastic decision RNG. This is not claimed to be the exact published learner.
