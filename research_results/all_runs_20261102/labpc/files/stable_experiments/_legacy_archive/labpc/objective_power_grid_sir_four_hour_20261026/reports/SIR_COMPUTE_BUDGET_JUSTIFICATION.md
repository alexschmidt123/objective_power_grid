# Primary compute budget justification

Four hours on the same LabPC RTX4090 is a practical matched-time ceiling, not a matched-data or equal-FLOP claim. Remove the mandatory1M episode allocation and24/48h optimization caps from the primary protocol. Native finite schedules remain algorithm-specific and require source verification before launching. All trainable phases and method-specific GPU setup/checkpoint overhead count. Random has no optimization; Fixed must disclose its complete offline design-search cost. Evaluation and external diagnostic/pilot overhead are separate reported expenditure. Never infer eligibility from optimization-only historical timing. The running1M study is historical supplemental evidence and continues independently.

Required ledger: charged seconds, complete native schedule yes/no, fresh episodes/observations, optimizer updates by component, batch/contrasts, likelihood evaluations, replay, pretraining, adaptive RK4 work, peak memory, excluded evaluation seconds, external setup/pilot cost, source/config/checkpoint SHA256, decision mean/median/p95 latency.
