# Fairness and limits

Primary: native method fidelity under same maximum4h allowance on LabPC4090. Not equal examples, FLOPs, optimizer counts or proven convergence. Native finite targets may complete earlier.2M sample-efficiency outputs and Ubuntu development stay separate. Historical failed/deleted artifacts do not prove success; partial checkpoints never used as full native outcomes. No EIG threshold or best test selection.

ScoreBED is explicitly time-adapted P1 with both native stages, not an all-restarts/full-schedule paper reproduction. New phase allocation follows pre-existing recovery safety evidence, not final EIG. Failed attempts counted; no automatic retry or clock reset.

Formal final bank is fresh, generated/hash-frozen before training; evaluated only after all eligible checkpoint/config freezes. Within-policy case MCSE, across-five-seed SD, paired case differences, across-seed paired t intervals and Holm3 principal comparisons are reported separately. No five-seed claims from partial data.18k MoE secondary requires actually reached checkpoints within4h and discloses LR/recipe differences; exposure-matched is not automatically sole-mechanism ablation.

MC standard errors condition on the shared fixed contrast bank; contrast-bank Monte Carlo variability and finite-sPCE bias are not included in those case-level MCSEs. No joint two-way variance claim is made by adding training-seed and case errors. Random five replicates measure policy RNG variation, never training-seed variability. CUDA-kernel active time/FLOPs are not inferred from exclusive-device charged wall time; unknown metrics remain unknown.

Development diagnostics use explicitly configured128-case native-baseline or64-case MoE banks; their actual array identities are recorded by CommonValidation. A shared seed alone does not make these the same bank as the earlier2000-case development bank. Do not pool those development scores as paired results. The NEW final bank is exactly2000cases/1Mcontrasts shared by ALL policies. GPU memory/power/utilization samples are in reports/gpu_samples.csv, separately from training clocks; historical missing memory stays unknown.
