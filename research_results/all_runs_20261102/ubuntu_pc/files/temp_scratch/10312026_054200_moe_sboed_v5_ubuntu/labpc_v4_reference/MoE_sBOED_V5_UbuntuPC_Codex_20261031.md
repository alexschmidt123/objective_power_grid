## E. V5-A — conditional contrastive information AUXILIARY (gated)

Only if measured saturation justifies it, add an **OFF-by-default** conditional-contrastive auxiliary while **retaining original full-horizon sPCE as primary**. Let `h_{t-1}` be the actually observed history and include carried physical state where the likelihood requires it. For simulated truth `theta0` and negatives `theta_ell^(t)` drawn independently from the *current* conditional posterior `p(theta | h_{t-1})`:

`C_t = log p(y_t | theta0, xi_t, h_{t-1}) - log[(sum_{ell=0}^L p(y_t | theta_ell^(t), xi_t, h_{t-1}))/(L+1)]`, and `J_cond = E[sum_{t=1}^T C_t]`.

The generating `theta0` is drawn from the prior jointly with history and is **conditionally posterior distributed given `h_{t-1}`**. With *exact* independent posterior negatives, each conditional contrastive expectation lower-bounds the corresponding conditional EIG and its sum bounds total EIG. **Approximate** negatives via particle reweighting/resampling are *not automatically a rigorous lower bound*: log sample proposal/weight corrections, ESS/coverage/duplicate rates, and reject unreliable batches. Do not resample from the prior pretending posterior; do not use observations from stage `t` or the future to construct its stage `t` negatives. Condition every likelihood on the correct carried state/history; never reset a stateful simulator between stages.

Optimize `J_total = J_full_sPCE + alpha_cond * J_cond` using native sign convention. Keep `alpha_cond=0` reproducing original V4 **exactly** (including RNG consumption and update count), and implement native-config versions for `diagnostics`, `V5-A`, `V5-B`, `V5-A+B`. Declare alpha and sampler overhead; consider an **initial small predeclared alpha** rather than a large tuning sweep; do not use final-test EIG for selection. Count **all** extra contrast likelihood evaluations, posterior sampling, simulation, optimizer work and GPU seconds. No claim that auxiliary is the main EIG estimator or that its improvement is guaranteed.

Unit tests: analytical Gaussian toy with known posterior, exact-vs-approximate sampler behavior, sample conditioning/no future leakage, per-stage ceiling, telescoping sPCE, numerical logsumexp stability, stateful likelihood consistency, finite and connected gradients, alpha=0 bit/tolerance parity, determinism and resource counters.
