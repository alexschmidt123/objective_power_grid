# Fixed diagnostic protocol
16 batches per frozen model; B16/L2000/T30, stages 0,5,15,25 repeated, RNG73101000+i*997. Same theta/noise/contrast draws for V4/V5; their own history policies differ. For on/off probes a model, RNG, histories and full Adam state are identical. Four independent one-step Adam clones per model; clones discarded, no training continuation.
Six fixed prefixes: one V4 and one V5 rollout at stages0,15,29; three independent pools per setting. Executed512/8moves versus approximate4096/32 reference; 128 negatives each, 16 subsample estimates per pool. Predictive comparison at actual action plus fixed probes; no exact oracle. Replicas, not particle count, determine MC error.
Gradient zero threshold1e-12; no cosine if either norm below threshold. Gradient domination means weighted auxiliary norm exceeds main norm. Conflict means cosine<0. No posterior certification inferred from resampled ESS. No new formal EIG. No reserved randomness. No coefficient search.
