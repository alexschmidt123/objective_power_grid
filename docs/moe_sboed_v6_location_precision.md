# Location finding precision study, T=30, seed 101

The outcome is a terminal learned-router policy scoring over 13 nats on the existing V2 primary bank. All policy weights start fresh. No teacher, previous checkpoint, auxiliary measurements or test-case parameter enters the policy. The 512 historical development trajectories are used only to diagnose sensor coverage.

Train two four-expert architectures with their own independently optimized uniform controls. All receive the same realized measurements, training budget and scalar objective. The first uses the observed-history head. The second uses the fused experts to weight actual past sensor coordinates (plus an origin option) and predict a bounded-domain displacement from their weighted center. Both select one physical action per stage. The comparison of the two absolute-head runs with the earlier K=8,000 absolute-head runs tests the auxiliary training signal. The comparison of the two architectures with the auxiliary enabled tests the head change. This study does not estimate the head change in isolation under plain sPCE.

The training objective is terminal sPCE with 8,000 prior contrasts plus 0.5 times a realized-path local D-optimality criterion. The auxiliary is half the log determinant of the identity plus the sum over the 30 observed designs of the outer products of the sensor mean's gradient in the four source coordinates, divided by the observation noise variance. The gradient is evaluated at the simulated truth with each realized design held fixed. The design policy receives gradients through its sampled trajectory. The identity is the standard normal prior precision. This proxy measures local sensitivity in four directions. Global ambiguity and source-label exchange prevent interpreting it, or the composite training objective, as an EIG estimate. The trainer logs the two components separately. The weight 0.5 is a pilot choice.

Each arm has 2,000 fresh episodes per update, up to 20,000 updates and a four-hour training cap. Record the terminal update count, resource use, run configuration, and training time for each arm. The development bank and original primary bank each have 2,000 cases and one million contrasts. After completing the four terminal development evaluations, select the architecture by the larger learned policy development EIG. An exact tie selects the observed-history head. Evaluate that learned policy and its matching Uniform on the canonical bank once, irrespective of which wins against Uniform. Report both results, paired case uncertainty, and whether the learned score strictly exceeds 13. One seed is a pilot. The canonical endpoint uses the unchanged frozen sPCE scorer and excludes the precision auxiliary. Prior canonical results informed the motivation for further development, so this bank has prior exposure.
