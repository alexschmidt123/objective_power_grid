# MoE-sBOED V6 design for location finding

**Research question.** With the same sequence of real measurements, training objective, and evaluation protocol, does a policy benefit from allowing four experts to interpret the observations differently and learning how to combine their features? Strong evidence would include gains over independently trained Uniform and SingleExpert, as well as competitive absolute full-horizon EIG against external methods. The 13.2-nat location T=30 target is an empirical aspiration.

## One decision

The hidden parameter is an unordered pair of 2D source locations. The action is one 2D sensor position. At stage zero the learned policy receives an empty history. After each measurement it encodes the actual design–signal pair, using the repository's location normalization. Each action depends only on the preceding measurements, stage, and trained weights.

1. A shared MLP (3 → 256 ReLU → 16) encodes each observed pair. Sum the token vectors and append the fraction of the horizon already used to form a 17-dimensional context. Retain the individual tokens. The sum provides the same type of history compression used by DAD. For static location finding, permuting the observed pairs preserves their joint likelihood in the source parameters under a policy that only sees past measurements. Each pair keeps its design and observation together.
2. Each expert maps the current context to a 16-dimensional vector. Dot products with the actual saved tokens, scaled by the square root of 16, give attention weights over past measurements. The expert uses their weighted mean together with the shared context as input to its 64/64 tanh MLP. Its output has 17 features. Before any observations, its reading is zero. An old observation can receive a different relative weight after a new measurement arrives. All experts use the same architecture and independent initial parameter draws.
3. One router maps the shared context through 32 tanh units to four logits. Softmax at temperature one gives the expert weights. The final logit layer starts at zero; the rest of the router and each expert are trainable. The distinction is important: attention **within an expert** chooses which observations to emphasize; the router **between experts** chooses which interpretations to emphasize.
4. Add the weighted sum of expert features to the shared context, as specified in the project proposal. One shared linear head emits two logits, and the native bounded location map produces the physical design in [-4,4]². The residual lets the head retain common evidence and creates a possible shortcut. Measure whether the experts change actions and EIG. Deployment uses zero hypothetical location probes and zero forward-model calls to choose an action. The environment generates the one observation after that choice.

All decision weights are initialized from scratch. A source, future outcome, pretrained belief provider, or training label cannot enter the policy input through this interface. Dense routing evaluates every active expert on every decision, so any speed or memory claim requires measurement.

## Baseline and MoE literature

| Method | Lesson and comparison |
|---|---|
| DAD | A policy maps observed history directly to a design. Full-horizon sPCE trains it offline. The repository's location sum encoder and feasible head provide the closest architectural control. [Foster et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/foster21a.html) |
| vsOED G-I | Its posterior supplies a variational training reward; its critic helps optimize its actor. At deployment the actor produces one design from the stage and history. The benefit of these training signals is a separate hypothesis from the benefit of a mixture. [Shen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2025.118068) |
| RL-sBOED | The native location implementation trains a stochastic design policy with contrastive information rewards and off-policy actor–critic updates. Preserve its deployed sampling rule, training budget and decision RNG. [Blau et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/blau22a.html) |
| ScoreBED | A score model trained offline supplies policy-gradient estimates. Charge score training and policy restarts; evaluate the deployed design policy with the common likelihood scorer. [Phillips et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v337/phillips26a.html) |
| Optimized Fixed and Random | Fixed learns a whole open-loop schedule; Random samples valid actions. They measure what can be achieved without observation-dependent adaptation. Random's five replicates vary the policy RNG. [Location baseline protocol](../configs/location_finding/location_native_baselines.yaml) |

Jacobs et al. motivate learning an input-dependent distribution over experts and allowing specializations to emerge. Here a differentiable gate mixes latent features; full-horizon sPCE supplies the learning signal. Puigcerver et al. distinguish assignment of tokens to expert slots from combining expert outputs. That distinction motivates separating measurement attention and expert routing; this design uses neither their Transformer layer nor their slot dispatch algorithm. Neither paper demonstrates an EIG gain for this application. [Adaptive Mixtures of Local Experts](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/jjnh91.pdf), [From Sparse to Soft Mixtures of Experts](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/79fea214543ba263952ac3f4e5452b14-Paper-Conference.pdf)

## Training and evidence

The first implementation and config target static location finding at T=30, seed 101. A fresh simulated truth generates all T observations. The native scorer replays that same realized design–observation sequence under the truth and L independent prior contrasts, sums log likelihoods over all T stages, and includes the truth once in the log-mean-exp denominator. The policy, experts, router and head maximize this terminal sPCE bound by pathwise gradients. There is one objective across the entire internal family. The cfg reuses the previous matched pilot budget: 2,000 episodes/update in microbatches of 500, 2,000 contrasts/episode, Adam at 5e-5, up to 20,000 updates or four hours per arm. Report actual updates, simulation and optimization costs, and any cap-limited checkpoint separately. With L=2,000 the training score ceiling is log(2001)=7.6014 nats. Track proximity to the ceiling and gradient norms before attributing weak learning to routing. A different estimator or contrast count would be a separate declared study.

- **Full and Uniform.** Train each separately from scratch. Uniform always gives each of four experts 1/4; its own observation attention remains learnable. Both arms have the same initial shared encoder, expert slots and head for a matched seed.
- **SingleExpert.** E1–E4 in this new config are independent training runs of the same one-expert architecture. Each starts with its corresponding fresh slot and trains its own encoder and head. Prespecify E1 as the primary SingleExpert control; include all four individually and their within-seed average as secondary. Four initializations within one seed do not provide four extra independent benchmark seeds. Historical extracted pathways retain their own provenance.
- **Mechanism.** Compare Full with a dense control matched on parameter count and measured active computation where feasible. On fresh development histories measure gate variation within a stage, expert feature similarity and gradients, and changes in actions when gates are fixed or expert features are zeroed. A uniform-gate inference intervention on Full is supplementary. Compare the contextual reader with a learned context-independent reader using the same training budget and seeds to test its specific contribution. Predeclare that control before running it. More even gate utilization alone is insufficient.
- **Evaluation.** Use a separate development bank for diagnosis. Keep the canonical T=30 bank, scorer, RNG and terminal checkpoint rule fixed. The primary score is the independently evaluated full-horizon sPCE lower bound in nats, with 2,000 paired cases and 1,000,000 contrasts; ceiling 13.8155 nats. Report per-case MCSE, between-seed SD and paired differences across seeds 101–105 with uncertainty. Test the two prespecified internal contrasts and report multiplicity treatment. Charge all training, selection and restarts, GPU and CPU usage, peak memory and synchronized batch-one design/episode latency and throughput. Single-seed pilot results cannot justify a general improvement claim.
- **Order of work.** The T=15 external baseline campaign has priority. This redesign has no queued training. For a subsequent T=15 MoE comparison, use the T=15 bank view already locked for all six baselines; the existing V6 primary evaluation CLI is T=30-specific. Time-dependent SIR histories, mixed grid action spaces, solver gradients and constraints need task-specific validation before extending the design. The present set encoder uses the static location likelihood symmetry.
