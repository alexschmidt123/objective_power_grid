# Frozen V2 routing opportunity diagnostic

No model training or source edits to frozen V2. Use the original successful iteration-16001 actor/runtime, checked against their existing SHA256 values by the validated loader.

Generate 24 independent original-V2 histories: eight at each observed-prefix length 3, 14 and 26. The original hidden parameters are discarded before conditional branching. Six interventions are compared: learned gates, uniform gates, and four expert-favoring mixtures with 0.625 on the favored expert and 0.125 elsewhere. Intervene at exactly one decision; thereafter every branch uses original learned V2 on its own newly generated observations. No repeated-selector deployment claim is made.

Conditional hidden parameters are sampled using a finite self-normalized importance approximation to the exact prefix posterior. Proposal: 10% standard-normal prior, 45% V2's frozen GMM belief, 45% the GMM with the two source labels permuted. Exact Gaussian observation likelihood (noise standard deviation 0.5), standard-normal prior and full proposal density are evaluated in float64. Each history has separate independent 131,072-particle selection and test proposals. Require ESS at least 256 and maximum normalized weight at most 0.05 in BOTH banks. Failure stops the diagnostic as INCONCLUSIVE_POSTERIOR_QUALITY; histories are not replaced. ESS does not prove full mode coverage or exact posterior sampling. Proposal symmetrization respects the exact source-permutation likelihood symmetry.

Selection: 32 posterior/noise futures per history, 4 million prior contrasts. Test: 64 independently drawn posterior/noise futures, independent 16-million-prior-contrast bank. Within each history/bank all six branches share hidden parameters, future noise and prior contrasts. Banks and seeds are independent across selection/test. Full T=30 float64 prior-sPCE terminal evaluation includes the observed prefix; the prefix's conditional information term is common to alternatives. This is a finite-contrast proxy for full-horizon EIG, not exact EIG. No reward/objective changes or optimizers are introduced.

Choose each history's mixture using ONLY selection returns. Also choose a global fixed mixture and one mixture per stage using ONLY selection returns. Primary result: heldout per-history-selected minus original learned routing, paired 95% t interval over 24 independent history units. Secondary descriptive intervals: selected minus selection-chosen global fixed, stage-specific, and uniform policies. No maximization of heldout values is reported as a realizable gain. Selection-bank winner counts alone are not evidence of conditional utility. Confidence intervals do not account for posterior approximation bias; 24 histories and this six-mixture family are a bounded exploratory diagnostic, not definitive proof that routing has or lacks room.

Wall budget: four hours, GPU lease required. Inconclusive/failed runs are retained, never silently retried with looser criteria. Save raw per-history branch scores, prefix histories, posterior diagnostics, manifests and compact reports in a new isolated scratch directory. Protect model weights and historical sources. Source is reviewed, committed and synchronized LabPC -> GitHub -> UbuntuPC before the full run. No Ubuntu computation is launched. Completion watcher publishes compact reports and verifies synchronized hashes; failures are reported explicitly.

Interpretation: positive independently validated history-dependent gain motivates subsequent supervised/native-EIG router development. A good fixed mixture motivates mixing calibration. A null result only constrains these mixtures and one-decision interventions under the stated approximation; it cannot prove no routing opportunity exists.
