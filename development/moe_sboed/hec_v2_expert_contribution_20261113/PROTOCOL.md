# Frozen V2 expert decision contribution diagnostic

No model training, objective changes or edits to frozen artifacts. Start from original V2 iteration 16001 using existing SHA-verified loader. Nine branches per conditional future: original, four individual expert removals at one decision, and four individual expert removals for every remaining decision. A removal zeros that expert's legacy features before coordinator, responses before response_gate, and first/response packets before fusion; the removed gate is zeroed and remaining gates renormalized. Use the previously tested all-consumer mask. Shared history and frozen SG/base action paths remain. Expert computations still execute; this is an information intervention, not sparse inference.

Generate 24 selection and 24 independent test histories, eight per observed-prefix length 3,14,26. Selection/test seeds differ by 100000; base seed 202611131901. Recorded generating truths are discarded before conditional branching. For each prefix, use the existing float64 exact-likelihood importance posterior correction: 131072 candidates from 0.1 prior +0.45 frozen belief GMM +0.45 source-permuted GMM. ESS>=256 and maximum weight<=0.05 required in all 48 histories. Failures produce an inconclusive diagnostic, with no replacement histories or relaxed criteria. This finite posterior approximation can miss modes despite acceptable ESS.

Selection: 32 posterior futures/history with 4M independent prior contrasts. Test:64 futures/history with independent16M prior contrasts. All nine branches within a history share theta, future noise and contrasts. Every branch advances using its own observations, preserving its observed prefix and zeroing unobserved fields initially. Score complete T30 trajectories with the existing float64 location-finding prior-sPCE evaluator. This is a finite-contrast proxy, not exact EIG. No true parameter or future observation enters regime features or actor inputs.

Save per-history action displacement after each removal, original gates, and exact per-branch terminal scores. Positive utility damage is original minus removed-expert score. Selection histories fit ONE shallow regime stump: choose one of stage fraction, frozen belief variance trace or gate entropy; threshold at selection quantiles .33,.5,.67, minimum leaf size n/6; each leaf selects the expert with largest mean persistent-removal damage. Choose stump/labels solely by selection returns. Also choose the globally most consequential expert from selection returns. Lock the fitted regime on disk before computing test returns.

Primary: on independent test histories, mean persistent-removal damage of regime-selected expert minus damage of selection-chosen global expert, paired95% t interval over24 test-history units. This tests conditional variation in expert necessity, NOT improved routing or deployment performance. Eight secondary mean-removal tests (four experts, single/persistent) have Holm8 adjusted p values. Regime leaf means are descriptive; feature/label differences alone do not establish specialization. Action magnitudes are reported without an arbitrary useful/useless cutoff.

Interpretation limitations: knockout damage may reflect head coadaptation, gating renormalization and out-of-distribution features, not intrinsic scientific competence. Individual removals cannot exhaustively establish redundancy or synergy. Positive conditional damage motivates targeted credit assignment; negligible decision effects motivate fusion analysis; a null result is inconclusive rather than proof that routing is impossible. One checkpoint and finite sample sizes do not establish cross-seed robustness.
