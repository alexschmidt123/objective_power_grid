# Novelty and future evidence

The proposed difference is joint learning of command-conditioned nonlocal discrimination/future-opportunity responses, preservation of useful inherited feature functions, and information-state-versus-stage coordination under the original full-horizon native objective. It is not a router-only change or a new EIG estimator. Commands change model computations before fusion; learned E2/E4 predictions extrapolate to separate probes/two-step targets rather than copying supplied analytic coordinates.

Prior art remains substantial: [DAD](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/foster21a.html), [vsOED](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10430), [RL-sBOED](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/blau22a.html), [ScoreBED](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08335), [MMoE](https://research.google/pubs/modeling-task-relationships-in-multi-task-learning-with-multi-gate-mixture-of-experts/), [PLE](https://doi.org/10.1145/3383313.3412236), [Expert Choice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09368), [DeepSeekMoE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06066), [FiLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07871), progressive/residual transfer and the project's SG/HEC methods. Soft gates, conditional computation, task preparation, anchors, likelihood ratios, Fisher information and Bayes reweighting are not new individually. No Step-DAD is included.

This is a scientifically motivated composition to test, not an established ICML contribution. Initial-function preservation, trained stage controls and response ablations improve identification but do not replace multiple seeds, model-approximation validation, capacity/time-matched controls or cross-domain evidence. Analytic semantic coordinates must not be misreported as learned competencies.

Later plans only: a predeclared independent-seed replication if the pilot warrants it; specialization×commander factorial and information/capacity controls; deterministic SIR ODE observation-time design; dynamic swing-equation inertia/damping probing. Existing port plans supply candidate models/units, not executed benchmark results. None of these campaigns launches automatically. No theorem is required to evaluate this pilot.
