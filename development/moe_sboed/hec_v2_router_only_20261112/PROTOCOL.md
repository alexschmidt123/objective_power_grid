# Frozen-head router refinement of HEC-v2

This is a training intervention on the existing successful V2, not a new router architecture. The testable hypothesis is that fixing the expert computations and action head gives the router a stationary decision map and improves its native information objective. This hypothesis is not an established explanation of previous results.

Both arms start from the identical iteration-16001 runtime, including replay, optimizer moments, critic, posterior, RNG and exploration schedule. `router_only` enables actor gradients only for `coordinator` and `response_gate` (25,924 parameters). History, commander, all experts/adapters and fusion/action head remain fixed. `ordinary` retains the parent's actor training flags. Native critic and posterior training, target-network Polyak updates and full-horizon variational objective remain unchanged. There are no auxiliary rewards or deployment-time optimization. This remains a dense soft MoE.

Each arm receives 2,000 additional native updates: 2,000 actor steps, 10,000 critic steps, 300,000 stage-posterior steps, 2 million new simulated episodes and 60 million new observations. Replay batch is 10,000 episodes. Arm order is router_only then ordinary; the complete parent RNG is restored independently for both. Initial randomness is matched; future histories/replay trajectories diverge as actions diverge. Total wall budget is four hours, including evaluation. A budget stop is incomplete, not a scientific result.

Only final checkpoints are evaluated, with no outcome-based selection. Primary comparison: router_only minus equal-budget ordinary continuation on 2,048 fresh prior/noise episodes, using a paired two-sided 95% t interval. Evaluation uses 16 million independently drawn prior contrasts and the existing validated full-T=30 float64 likelihood evaluator. Case seed 202611129101, contrast seed 202611129102. Secondary comparisons: frozen original V2, original uniform V2 and refined router's own uniform ablation. Secondary intervals are exploratory and unadjusted. A finite-contrast lower bound and one inherited training lineage do not establish exact EIG or multi-seed robustness.

Before training, verify bitwise forward equivalence after freezing, optimizer-state membership, nonzero finite native-loss gradients in both router components and absent frozen-parameter gradients on actual saved histories at t=0,14,29. During training check native step counts and finite gradients. After each arm verify all frozen actor parameters and model buffers are bitwise unchanged and router weights changed. Historical artifacts and source hashes are verified after the completed run. Original checkpoints are never overwritten. GPU leases wait for idle hardware; running jobs are never interrupted.

Implement/source-sync on LabPC before launching. Training and large outputs remain outside Git. On completion, create compact reports, scoped commit/push through the approved sync operator, ff-only update Ubuntu and verify all commit hashes. A failed training/evaluation/sync is reported explicitly; no automatic redesign, retraining or additional compute budget follows.
