# Specialist preparation tasks

Shared Phase A uses 4,096 calibration prefixes and 512 held-out prefixes from distinct trajectories in the saved SG replay. Only past designs and observations form inputs. No replay latent values are read. Stages are sampled uniformly from 0..29. This replay was generated by historical SG behavior with its exploration and inherited buffer; it is not a new final-test bank.

Half the preparation commands are zero; half are uniform in [-1,1]^20. Both arms receive identical prepared features and readouts. There are 300 Adam updates of batch 256, lr 0.001. The four standardized MSE losses are summed only during preparation. Scale is calibration target SD clipped below at 0.1. Validation does not select architecture, schedule or checkpoint.

1. Geometry: from approximate Gaussian-mixture tokens and region attention, predict four mixture CDF probabilities at its attended mean, four log(1+variance) coordinates and six parameter correlations. This preserves access to uncertainty, concentration and dependencies. Mixture representations can encode multimodality, but no claim that moments uniquely identify every mode is made.
2. Discrimination: use the actual log-signal at commanded provisional/plus/minus designs and 64 belief quadrature nodes. Predict three predictive log-signal means, three log(1+expected pairwise Gaussian KL) values and three standardized third moments. The exact known likelihood is used, not fitted by a surrogate. Expected pairwise component KL is not predictive-mixture KL or EIG.
3. Sensitivity: use the analytically correct expected local Fisher matrix and commanded parameter direction. Predict four log(1+Fisher eigenvalues), four squared coordinates of its weakest eigenvector, and directional information. Eigenvector labels can be unstable near repeated eigenvalues; they are preparation targets, not unquestioned competency ground truth.
4. Sequential opportunities: predict a three-design vector of one-step information-increment plus remaining-value differences, with two predictive replicas from the frozen approximate GMM and common noise across probes. The next history is updated with newly simulated observations, then the SG continuation and frozen critic supply bootstrap values. Terminal queries use terminal variational density. This is a noisy approximate-belief bootstrap target, not exact expected full-horizon EIG or merely a copy of scalar Q(h,a).

Distinct roles here are deliberately assigned as requested, while command selection and collaboration are learned. Improved information gain, genuine complementarity and benefits of those assignments must be demonstrated rather than inferred from architecture. No preparation loss continues into native EIG phases.
