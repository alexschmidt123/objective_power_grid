# Prospective protocol

One candidate and one matched heterogeneous control start from the successful SG runtime at iteration 12001. Parent critic, learned posteriors, targets, 1M replay and sampling RNG are retained. The compatible history-encoder parameters/Adam moments are retained; new shape-incompatible experts, commander and fusion receive fresh Adam state. Frozen SG policy/belief/critic providers are shared in specification but copied safely as buffers.

Phase A: one shared 300-step supervised preparation, batch 256, 4096 training/512 validation prefixes. Both arms get the identical prepared state and identical charge for preparation. No native optimizer step or new real trajectory is used here. Two synthetic future observations per each of three design probes are separately counted. Validation is descriptive; no tuning/restarts.

Phase B: 200 native actor–critic EIG updates, train commands/coordination/fusion, freeze live history/experts. Phase C: 1800 native updates jointly training history, all four experts, commands, coordination and fusion. Readouts remain frozen. Total 2000 native continuation updates per arm, target iteration 14001. This is the bounded continuation scale used in SG, not a fresh 10001-step campaign. Native reference full training was 10001 updates; inherited SG already used 2000 extra.

Each complete arm: 2M new episodes, 60M observation simulations, actor 2000/critic 10000/posterior 300000 steps. Batch 10000, replay 1M, five critic and five updates of 30 posterior nets per iteration, Polyak 0.1. Saved learning rates, .9999 decay, exploration and decay continue unchanged. No extra initial replay. Both use gamma1 and the proper posterior safeguard inherited from SG. No continuing specialist loss, greedy reward, cost penalty or sPCE training.

Control then candidate sequentially on LabPC. Acquire existing shared GPU locks; queue persistently if busy. Duplicate-safe one-shot launch. Preparation plus both arms plus evaluation: graceful 21600s, owned TERM22200s, hard22800s. Evaluation cap3600s inside graceful limit. Stop on correctness/nonfinite/resource failure. No extensions, architecture sweeps, extra seeds, or automatic SIR/grid campaigns.

Independent development bank seed202611099104: 2000 T30 trajectories, 1M prior negatives, positive included exactly once in denominator of 1,000,001. Fixed original scorer SHA is preserved. FP64 likelihood, native policy precision, batch16/chunk16384. Primary paired candidate-control two-sided t95CI and p<.05. Secondary SG/Option/vsOED/Collaborative Holm4, with unadjusted CIs clearly labeled. Other baseline comparisons descriptive. No final-test data and no cross-bank pairing. Single-seed CIs cover trajectory Monte Carlo variation only, not training-seed variability or estimator bias.

Diagnostics: 512 independent cases, first256 calibration/last256 validation; seed202611099106, donors202611099107. Commands zero/stage-mean/stage-donor-shuffle Holm3. Coordination ordinary linear projection/uniform/stage mean Holm3. Four expert removals Holm4 with OOD caveat. Stage summaries, ridge1 predictors and rival choices use calibration only. Expert tasks at stages3/12/24; case-clustered differences and Holm4 per competency/retention family. Teachers use a separate two-replica seed202611099109. Future observations are always resimulated after policy changes.

Success requires positive matched EIG difference and supporting useful competency/command/coordination evidence. A positive EIG result without that evidence is partial. Never equate distinct representations or variable commands with useful specialization. Report all negative results and effective capacity/compute limitations.
